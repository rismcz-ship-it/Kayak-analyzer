## Variant: CAD / Workbench Dense

### Design stance
Maximize raw information density and engineering control in a classic 4-quadrant CAD layout, ensuring all inputs, 3D viewport, hydrostatic readouts, and stability curves remain visible simultaneously.

### Key choices
- **Layout**: 4-quadrant docking layout (Top status bar, Left payload/solver tree, Center primary 3D viewport, Right hydrostatic metrics, Bottom GZ dock).
- **Typography**: Monospace and technical sans-serif for high numerical legibility.
- **Color**: Dark slate background with cyan/blue accents and high-contrast green/red vector markers.
- **Interaction**: Direct parameter adjustment, real-time live vector recalculation, multi-axis camera toggles.

### Trade-offs
- **Strong at**: Deep technical workflows, rapid micro-adjustments, and eliminating navigation overhead.
- **Weak at**: High cognitive load on beginners, tighter viewport area on smaller laptop displays.

### Best for
- Hydrodynamics engineers and CAD designers who want complete control over equilibrium calculations without leaving the primary screen.
